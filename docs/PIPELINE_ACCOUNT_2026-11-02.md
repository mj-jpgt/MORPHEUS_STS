## 1. Discovery cohort and raw inputs
- Meylan 2022 ccRCC (Immunity; PMID 35231421), 24 Visium sections INT1..INT24, one section per patient ("Patient N" in HEST metadata; protocol: "1 sample = 1 patient").
- Source layout: HEST-1k mirror /lambda/nfs/geeg/biorag3_persistent_20260711/morpheus_substrate_mirror/hest/ccrcc: per sample `_st.h5ad` (counts, 17,943 genes), `_patches.h5` (224x224 RGB tiles; source window 245 px at 0.4573 um/px = 112 um, bicubic to 224), `_metadata.json`, `_cellvit_seg.parquet` (+geojson; HEST-shipped CellViT nuclei), WSI `INT*.tif` (INT21: 16,878 x 17,128 px, 40x, 0.4577 um/px embedded, 8-level pyramid).
- Visium geometry: spot diameter 55 um, centre pitch 100 um; INT21 has 4,975 spots under tissue; 73,978 scored spots cohort-wide.
- Chemistry (catalogue `subseries`, verified for all 24): INT1-INT12 = fresh-frozen poly-A Visium (36,601 genes, 1,084-2,580 spots per section; Patients 24..13); INT13-INT24 = FFPE probe-based Visium (17,943-gene human probe set, 3,206-4,975 spots per section; Patients 12..1). The cohort is therefore 12 frozen + 12 FFPE sections, treated as one "visium" platform with no chemistry covariate; the FFPE sections carry roughly 70% of the spots. The gene panel used is the intersection over all 24 samples (so the FFPE probe panel bounds it). Patient numbering is reversed against the id (INT1 = Patient 24, INT21 = Patient 4). The external-replication protocol text (ccrcc_external_replication_v03.json cohorts.ccRCC.discovery_cohort "Visium fresh-frozen") is inaccurate for INT13-INT24 and must be corrected in the paper. The evidence-card section INT21 is FFPE.

## 2. Molecular preprocessing (hm)
- Spot filter: depth = obs.total_counts (else row sum); keep depth >= min_depth (load_study default 200); a sample with < 10 such spots is dropped (hm._read_sample).
- Normalisation: x_ig = log1p(c_ig * 1e4 / depth_i) on stored sparse entries (hm._logcpm_from_counts). "log-CPM" in the code means counts per 10,000. The depth in the denominator is the UMI sum over the study's COMMON gene panel (intersection of symbols across samples, floored at 1), whereas the min_depth filter uses the all-gene obs.total_counts.
- Gene identifiers: symbols kept; Ensembl ids mapped through GENCODE v47 (store external/gene_annotation/gencode_v47_ensg_to_symbol.tsv), sample dropped if < 50% map; duplicate symbols dropped.
- Highly variable genes: top 768 by variance of log-CPM (E[x^2] - E[x]^2) computed on support (D_P) rows only (hm.select_hvg; protocol candidate_variables). Operators use the top 128 support-variance HVGs (hm.OPERATOR_TOP_GENES).
- Gene-set scores: mean log1p-CPM over the set's genes present in the FULL panel, computed before the HVG cut (measurement_bank.score_gene_sets): 41 Gavish meta-programmes ("programmes:mp:<name>") and 50 MSigDB Hallmark sets 2024.1.Hs ("programmes:hallmark:HALLMARK_<name>"). Raw-gene candidates ("raw_genes:<symbol>") are the gene's log-CPM.
- Within-patient standardisation ("within-patient z"): every response and every column: subtract the patient mean, divide by the patient SD (ddof 0, floored at 1e-8), each column by its own values only (hest._within_patient_standardise; ch._within_std; protocol response_transform). This is how patient identity enters every model: there are no patient dummies, the centring is the per-patient intercept. In the registered discovery run this happens in two layers: the loader first removes a ridge-shrunken per-patient mean from the HVG log-CPM and programme views (hm.patient_fixed_effect_residual, alpha = 1: beta_p = sum_p / (n_p + 1)), then the runner standardises each candidate block per patient (mean 0, sd 1) and finally z-scores with the proposal-set (D_P) mean and sd, which are frozen and applied to the held-out fold (run_general_discovery_packet._within_standardise). Characterisation and cards use the single-step within-patient z.
- Nuisance covariate: log1p(depth), within-patient standardised (ch.Ctx.depth). Platform and study one-hots only when an episode pools sections of different platforms or studies (not the case for Meylan).

## 3. Spatial graph and operators
- Neighbour graph: per section, k nearest spots by cKDTree on full-resolution pixel coordinates, self excluded, -1 padded, never crossing sections (hm.knn_neighbors_kdtree); k = 6 (first hex ring) and k = 18 (first two rings).
- Transforms (protocol.transforms): own; nbr6 = mean over the 6 NN; nbr18 = mean over the 18 NN; contrast = own - nbr6. A spot with no neighbours keeps its own value.
- het (local heterogeneity): population variance of the value over the spot's 18 neighbours, var_i = mean_{j in N18(i)} v_j^2 - (mean_j v_j)^2 (vals.neighbour_moments on the 18-NN adjacency; the spot itself is not in its own adjacency).
- grad (local gradient magnitude): for each spot, least-squares plane v_j ~ a (x_j - x_i) + b (y_j - y_i) + c over its 18 neighbours (pseudo-inverse of the design; >= 3 neighbours required, else NaN), grad_i = sqrt(a^2 + b^2) (vals.gradient_operator; ch.Ctx.apply_op). Units: value per full-res pixel.
- rings: graph rings 1..3 of the 6-NN graph (hops); ring_k mean = mean over spots exactly k hops away; ringS_k / ringE_k restrict the mean to stromal-immune-dominant / epithelial-dominant spots; nbrS6/nbrE6/nbrS18/nbrE18 = neighbour means restricted to one compartment (hm_v08 graph_rings, compartment_mean, COMPARTMENTS). Rows where any operator of the family is undefined (no neighbour of the class) are dropped, never imputed (vals.discovery_row_mask).
- Operators are applied to every non-excluded programme score and the top-128 HVGs (protocol.operators).
- Compartment labels: CellViT PanNuke dominance from the architecture table (epithelial_dominant, stromal_immune_dominant).

## 4. Image pipeline
- H-Optimus-0 (bioptimus/H-optimus-0; timm ViT, CLS global pool, 1536-d). Input 224x224 uint8 -> float, normalised with mean (0.707223, 0.578729, 0.703617), std (0.211883, 0.230117, 0.177517) (dbench/scripts/hest_fm_embed_hoptimus.py). Tiles are the HEST patches (112 um field). Multi-field embeddings at 112 / 448 / 896 um also exist in the store (code/ws3_hoptimus_reconstruction_20260824) for the H&E student work.
- Morphology view in discovery (protocol.morphology_view): cosine similarity of the frozen spot embedding, standardised per dimension on the fold's covered D_P spots, to the 33 kept centres of a k = 64 k-means dictionary built on 12 meta-training studies (centres kept if populated by >= 20 spots in >= 3 studies); columns morph_dictionary_v03:morph:kNN; targets morph:kNN. Only the residual family used this view as candidates; none of the four highlighted relations (G3, G4, G5, G7) contain an image term.
- CellViT (HEST-shipped segmentation for Meylan; our own runs for external cohorts): PanNuke classes Neoplastic, Connective, Epithelial, Inflammatory, Dead. A nucleus belongs to a spot if its polygon centroid lies within the spot's own radius (spot_diameter_fullres / 2); per spot: count, mean nuclear area, class fractions (hest.load_cellvit_summary). Architecture table architecture_v1_20261023 (cache/hest_multi/architecture): neoplastic / inflammatory / connective / epithelial fractions, n_nuclei, segmented flag, dominance labels (epithelial_dominant, stromal_immune_dominant, normal_appearing), gland-architecture and budding descriptors, inferred-CNV score, distance to neoplastic, invasive front, um_per_px per section (0.457-0.459). Spots without nuclei get the section mean fraction in characterisation (registered fallback).
- Deconvolution: NNLS v2 (cx_inputs_v1_20261026) onto a kidney reference with types Tumor, B_cell, Macrophage, Mast, Monocyte, NK_cell, Plasma, T_cell, Endothelial (several), Pericyte, vSMC, Collecting_duct, Distal_tubule, Loop_of_Henle, Proximal_tubule, Urothelium (ch.Ctx.composition_block). Reference source: see inventory.

## 5. Targets (response-free, fixed before any outcome)
- Programme targets: Gavish meta-programmes ranked by median within-section Moran's I (k = 6 row-standardised graph; I = (n/S0) sum_ij w_ij z_i z_j / sum_i z_i^2) over all 24 sections (hm.section_moran_i; runs/development/sprint_v06/blind_targets_meylan2022_ccrcc.json): IG (1), Hypoxia (2), EMT-I (3), Glutathione (4), Interferon/MHC-II (I), Protein maturation, Interferon/MHC-II (II), Metal-response.
- heterogeneity_gradient family targets: het: and grad: of IG, Hypoxia, EMT-I, Glutathione (8 targets). compartment_context and ring_decay targets: the 8 own-spot programmes. residual family targets: resid:G12, G18, G7, G17, G20, G1, G16, G19.
- Residual programmes: residual of the top-768-variance genes' log-CPM after log depth, composition and the 41 Gavish programmes (residual_programmes.fit, parallel-analysis rank, k = 20), fitted once on all spots, ranked by Moran's I (hm_v08.residual_targets).
- Exclusions per target (protocol.target_exclusions): the target programme under every transform; its member genes; every programme with gene-set overlap coefficient |A n B| / min(|A|,|B|) >= 0.2; any column with within-patient |r| >= 0.9 to the target or to its nbr6 mean on D_P; depth, patient, platform.

## 6. The relation model v03_rff (rv)
Notation: y = within-patient z target; C = nuisance columns; Z = k candidate columns (within-patient z); patients p = 1..P.
- Baseline f_B: ridge of y on [standardised C, 1] with penalty alpha chosen from {0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000} by leave-one-patient-out SSE, solved from per-patient Gram matrices: beta_{-p} = (sum_q G_q - G_p + alpha I)^-1 (sum_q b_q - b_p), G_q = Z_q^T Z_q, b_q = Z_q^T y_q. Residuals: r_s (full support fit), r_cv (LOPO cross-fitted), r_q (query rows from the full support fit) (rv.fit_baseline).
- Features: Z standardised by support mean/SD; bandwidth = median pairwise Euclidean distance among 256 support rows (rv.median_bandwidth); Phi(z) = [ sqrt(2/256) cos(z/bw . omega + phi) over 256 random directions (omega ~ N(0, I_k), phi ~ U(0, 2pi), seed 20261021 + 1000 k) | z (k linear terms) | 1 ] (rv.features). So f_S is a Gaussian-kernel random-feature ridge plus linear terms: 256 + k + 1 coefficients.
- Fit: ridge of the baseline residual r on Phi with alpha from {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10} chosen by LOPO; per-patient Gram/moment sums, same closed form as above; LOPO SSE_p = ||r_cv,p||^2 - 2 beta_{-p}.c_p + beta_{-p}^T G_p beta_{-p}. Support gain g_sup = sum_p (||r_cv,p||^2 - SSE_p) / sum_p SST_p with SST_p = sum (y - mean_p y)^2 (rv._fit).
- Query (held-out) gain: full-support coefficients beta; pred_q = Phi(z_q) beta; per query patient gain_p = ||r_q,p||^2 - ||r_q,p - pred_q,p||^2; G = sum_p gain_p / sum_p SST_q,p, i.e. R^2(f_B + f_S) - R^2(f_B) on query rows against the within-patient centred target; also averaged over 3 random two-thirds subsets of query patients (g). Patient-bootstrap intervals resample the per-patient (gain_p, SST_p) pairs: 2000 draws in characterisation and external replication.
- Decomposition: G_HO = gain(joint RFF on S) - max over proper partitions of S of gain(block-wise RFF, each block its own 256 RFF map); higher_order flag = G_HO patient-bootstrap CI lower bound > 0 and full-model R^2 > 0 (protocol.higher_order_flag).
- Baselines (rv docstring; protocol.explanation_decomposition): B_nuis = depth; B_known = depth + all Hallmark/Gavish scores after the target's exclusions + three composition variables (CellViT neoplastic / inflammatory / connective fractions); B_decomp = best proper partition.

## 7. Discovery protocol v08 (registered 2026-10-26)
- Outer split: 5 grouped patient folds (seed 20260926); each fold in turn is the replication set D_R, the other four the proposal set D_P; HVG selection, z statistics and programme ranking on D_P only.
- Proposers (protocol.proposers; run README "learned_search + support_screen_search + classical_pair, interleaved, K = 64"): learned_search (frozen checkpoint runs/development/learned_search_v03/release/frozen.pt, sha 13c36faa...), support_screen_search (search_v03.SupportScreen: singles ranked by their exact support LOPO gain g_sup under v03_rff, pairs by the mean of their singles' support gains), classical_pair (EBM GA2M pairwise terms; gradient boosting + Friedman H). Beam per (target, fold): K = 64 fitted sets = 26 singles + 26 pairs + 8 triples (grown from the best quarter of pairs by the top-32 singles) + 4 quadruples (from the top 2 triples); admissibility enforces the family's context requirement; final rank by exact support gain g_sup; the first 32 unique hypotheses per proposer are frozen, proposers merged by interleaved rank, no cross-validated re-ranking. Set size k = 1..4. Candidate design widths: ring_decay 2,761 columns (88 programmes + 729 genes + 216 base x 9 ring operators), residual 843 (91 + 719 + 33 morphology).
- Candidate identity for internal dedup: sha256 of [family, target, feature form, sorted variable names]. External dedup groups G1-G8: among replicated candidates with the same target, union-find on Jaccard(variable sets) >= 0.5; best member = largest nuisance gain.
- Passing fold: held-out full-model R^2 > 0, gain patient-bootstrap CI lower bound > 0, within-patient permutation p <= 0.05. Internal pass: passing in >= 3 of 5 folds, mean gain over passing folds >= 0.01, structured nulls p <= 0.05 in every passing fold (199 draws each; spatial_block = within-patient 6x6 block swap of the response, within_patient_perm, interaction_break = within-patient block swap of one constituent for |S| >= 2; p = (1 + #null >= observed)/(draws + 1)), BH q <= 0.10 within family. Budget: 50 frozen hypotheses per family; pooling scores every frozen hypothesis in every fold.
- Families: heterogeneity_gradient (>= 1 het/grad term + >= 1 other), compartment_context (>= 1 nbrS/nbrE term + 1 other), ring_decay (>= 1 ring term + 1 other; post hoc ring1/2/3 readout), residual_programme_targets (full vocabulary incl. morphology dictionary, transforms own/nbr6/nbr18/contrast). Row loss: ring_decay keeps only rows where every ring/compartment operator is defined (21,603 of 73,978 in fold 0; 52,375 undefined); the residual family keeps all 73,978.
- Bootstrap counts differ by stage: per-fold gain CI 200 held-out-patient draws (V03_N_BOOT); characterisation 2,000; external pooled 2,000.
- Null-p floor: with 199 draws the smallest attainable p is 0.005; the sampled receipts all sit at that floor, so the family BH q (computed on the worst of the three null p) saturates near 0.005 and does not rank the 161 internal passes; the gain over B_known is the discriminating quantity (28 of 161 pass B_known; 0 pass B_decomp).
- Funnel (funnel_aggregate.json, 2026-11-01): 3,990 candidate groups; 200 frozen (50 per family); 161 internal (compartment 50, ring 50, residual 50, het/grad 11); external family BH (masked variant): 111 tested, 57 replicated; 8 dedup groups. Protocol min_gain 0.01 governs the funnel; receipts also carry a legacy validation_rule value 0.02 (unresolved which flag it drives).
- Family colours in the figures: heterogeneity #E69F00, compartment #0072B2, residual #009E73, ring #CC79A7.

## 8. Characterisation ladder (ch.characterise_relation)
- Rows: discovery row mask (operators defined) and finite columns; n_patients 24.
- Rungs: C0 = depth; C1 = depth + K (known: all 91 programme scores minus the target's exclusions + the 3 composition variables; dbench/downstream/blind.known_columns_v03); C2 = C1 + COMP (all deconvolution types + CellViT 3 fractions + 5 grouped fractions tumour/immune/endothelial/mural/tubule + nbr6 means of all of these); CT = C1 + TECH (haemoglobin HBA1/HBA2/HBB mean log-CPM, number of valid 6-neighbours); C3 = C1 + COMP + TECH + own-spot versions of the constituents. Each rung: LOPO gain of the relation over that baseline, per-patient contributions, 2000 patient bootstraps, p = (1 + #boot <= 0)/(2001), MDE = 2.8 SE.
- Further rows: composition target-permutation null within patient x composition strata (199 draws), G_MS claim-matched and own-spot reductions, het matched null (199 draws), technical row.

## 9. External replication v03 (frozen before any external value was read)
- Cohorts: Li 2022 (Mendeley 10.17632/g67bkbnhhg.1; 16 sections, 8 patients, fresh-frozen whole transcriptome), GSE250163 (5 FFPE probe-based sections), Zenodo 14620362 (3 FFPE kidney sections KC1-KC3; TLS annotations used only in the phenotype layer).
- Same arithmetic by gene symbol: log1p CP10k over the section's panel; gene-set scores over the genes that entered discovery; operators rebuilt on each external section with the same graph construction (6/18 NN within section, not a physical radius); a candidate or target with any discovery gene absent is not testable (no imputation). No rank transform in v03; FFPE and fresh-frozen receive identical arithmetic and per-cohort gains are reported.
- Standardisation on the external cohort: within-patient centring and division by the pooled within-patient SD computed on the external cohort. Nuisance N = ridge on standardised log depth, cross-fitted by patient (K = min(5, patients), seed 20261016).
- No refit: f_S applied with its frozen coefficients, bandwidth and standardisation. Gain G = R^2(N + f_S) - R^2(N) from per-patient sufficient statistics pooled over every testable patient of every cohort (16 patients).
- Pass: bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0 (2000 draws, patients resampled within cohort strata, seed 20261021) AND dominant-term direction agreement >= 0.60 of external patients AND family-wide BH q <= 0.05 (family = all internally replicated findings; dbench/scripts/aggregate_v08_external_family_bh_v01.py, "masked" variant). Reported, not gating: gain over B_known, B_decomp, per-cohort gains.

## 10. Cards (characterisation_v10/cards; cards_v10.py)
- state = f_S fitted and evaluated on all discovery rows (decode_v10.state_of; support = query); nuisance_residual = r_s of the depth baseline; relation_residual = nuisance_residual - state. In-sample; the ladder carries the held-out numbers. (Panel a is being re-rendered with the leave-one-patient-out prediction for INT21.)
- UMAP: 20,000 spots drawn at random (seed 0); input matrix = [relation constituents Z, target, CellViT 3 fractions, state], within-patient standardised; umap.UMAP(n_neighbors = 30, min_dist = 0.3, random_state = 0). Because the state and target are inputs, UMAP separation of the high-state lobe is partly built in and is never used as evidence.
- Composition sources: CellViT fractions for Meylan come from the HEST-1k-released CellViT segmentation (PanNuke taxonomy); CPTAC and the Zenodo external slides were segmented by us with the cellvit package (HIPT x40 checkpoint, PanNuke taxonomy, wsi_mpp 0.5). Deconvolution reference: GSE159115 (ccRCC + benign kidney scRNA), NNLS v2 on per-type top-50 markers scaled by their maximum, immunoglobulin genes excluded, 19 types.
- Crops: 250 um regions, composition-matched high/low pairs by Mahalanobis distance on CellViT fractions + deconvolved groups (characterise.regions / matched_high_low), 6 pairs per card.
